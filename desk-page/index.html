Last fill: red when a fill came after the window closed. Stop orders: stop orders used; "trailed" counts each move, "pulled" means cancelled with no replacement. Off plan: positions with no plan card written before the entry. Added lower: bought more below the running average. Held loser down: a losing position whose later exits were lower than the first. Gave back: a scaled-out winner whose later exits were lower than the first. Runner paid: later exits higher than the first. R:R: average win ÷ average loss, with the win rate it needs to break even. R on a position: P&L ÷ the max loss on its plan card, or the default share of premium when none was written. Biggest risk: the most premium in one position, as a multiple of the daily stop.
